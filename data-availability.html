Data Availability

Data is available for all of our research projects. This includes quantum trajectory data and raw data files for experiments. Please contact the lead author on each paper for requested data. We are also happy to share our computer code (LabView) and design files upon request. Please send requests to murch@physics.wustl.edu


Specific data repositories are linked below:

John Templeton Foundation Grant No. 61835 - Energetic cost of measurements using quantum, coherent, and thermal light. Zip file with data and analysis


John Templeton Foundation Grant No. 61835 - Quantum energetics of a non-commuting measurement. Data will be linked when the paper is submitted.
